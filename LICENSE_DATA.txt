The religion shares in the resources/ directory come from the CIA World Factbook
("People and Society: Religions"), a US government work in the public domain,
as archived in factbook/factbook.json (CC0 1.0). About 25 countries have rows
written by hand for this library (resources/raw/religion_curated.tsv); those
rows and the grouping of religions are released with the library under the
MIT License (see LICENSE.MIT). The Pew Research Center's estimates, as
republished by Our World in Data, were used only to check the shares and are
not part of this repository. See doc/data.md.
