Religion shares (resources/*/religion.tsv) come from the CIA World Factbook
("People and Society: Religions"), a US government work in the public domain,
as archived in factbook/factbook.json (CC0 1.0). About 25 countries have rows
written by hand for this library (resources/raw/religion_curated.tsv).

The sex, age and country factors of the activity classes (rows with basis
"eurostat HETUS 2000/2010" in resources/*/affinity.tsv) are derived from
Eurostat's Harmonised European Time Use Surveys, dataset tus_00age
(https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/time-use-surveys). Source: Eurostat. Reuse
is authorised under Eurostat's reuse policy (Commission Decision
2011/833/EU) with acknowledgement of the source.

The hobby and genre catalogue (interests.tsv), the age bands, and all other
factor rows (basis "curated") were written for this library and are released
with it under the MIT License (see LICENSE.MIT), as are the grouping of
religions and the mapping from activity classes to hobbies. The Pew Research
Center's estimates, as republished by Our World in Data, were used only to
check the religion shares and are not part of this repository.

See doc/data.md.
